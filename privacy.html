Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vinh Le Advisory LLC ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website, call a phone number we operate, or receive text messages from us.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as name, phone number, email address, and service address.
  • Call information, including caller phone number, call time and duration, and, where permitted, call recordings and transcripts.
  • Text message content and delivery information.
  • Basic website usage data that hosting providers log automatically.

How we use information

To answer and route calls, take messages, schedule or confirm service requests, send appointment and service-related notifications, respond to inquiries, improve our services, and meet legal obligations.

Calls and recordings

Calls to numbers we operate may be answered by an automated AI voice agent and may be recorded and transcribed for quality, message-taking, and record-keeping purposes. Callers are informed at the start of a call where required by law.

Voice and SMS consent

Our SMS program, Vinh Le Advisory LLC customer alerts, is joined by texting ALERTS to +1 (727) 477-9925 (see Text Alerts).

By providing your phone number and agreeing to receive communications, you consent to receive calls and text messages from Vinh Le Advisory LLC, including automated messages, related to your inquiry or service request. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of text messages at any time; reply HELP for help, or email vqle@icloud.com. You may also ask us to stop calling you at any time.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile phone numbers and text messaging opt-in data and consent are not sold, rented, or shared with any third parties, except service providers (such as our messaging provider) that deliver messages on our behalf.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, telephony, AI processing, email, and hosting providers) under obligations to protect it, with the business you were trying to reach, or when required by law.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above and use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing vqle@icloud.com.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the revised version on this page.
