Who we are

Vinh Le Advisory LLC is an independent advisory firm founded by Vinh Le. We work directly with business owners to apply modern technology, especially AI-powered phone answering, to everyday operational problems such as missed calls, after-hours requests, and scheduling.

Services

AI voice answering

An AI phone agent that answers calls on behalf of your business, greets callers by your company name, answers common questions from information you provide, takes messages, and routes urgent requests to your on-call staff. A voicemail backup captures messages if the system is ever unavailable.

Business advisory

Guidance on customer communication, call handling, intake workflows, and adopting AI tools responsibly, tailored to the size and needs of your business.

Setup and support

We configure the phone line, scripts, and notifications with you, review results together, and adjust over time.
